# ADR-028: Filter Differential Pressure Monitoring

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Silt and algae clog drip irrigation filters, starving crops and risking pump deadheading.

## Decision
Ingest inlet and outlet pressure sensors, triggering alerts and procurement when differential pressure exceeds 0.8 bar.

## Consequences
Predictive maintenance of secondary filtration cartridges before dripline clogging occurs.
