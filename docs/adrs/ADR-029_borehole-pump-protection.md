# ADR-029: Borehole Pump Dry-Run Protection

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Running borehole submersible pumps dry causes motor seizure and catastrophic pump failure.

## Decision
Integrate hydrostatic level transmitters with preflight pump actuation interlocks halting runs if water level is below minimum threshold.

## Consequences
100% prevention of dry-run cavitation and motor overheating.
