# Black Vertisol / Cotton Soil Dynamics

- **Field Capacity (FC)**: 48.0% volumetric
- **Permanent Wilting Point (PWP)**: 28.0% volumetric
- **Available Water Capacity (AWC)**: 20.0% volumetric
- **Infiltration Rate**: 2.0 mm/hr

## Soil Management Notes
High shrink-swell smectite clay. Deep cracks develop when dry; infiltration drops to near zero when wet.
