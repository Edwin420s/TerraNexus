# Clay Loam Soil Water Dynamics

- **Field Capacity (FC)**: 34.0% volumetric
- **Permanent Wilting Point (PWP)**: 18.0% volumetric
- **Available Water Capacity (AWC)**: 16.0% volumetric
- **Infiltration Rate**: 8.0 mm/hr

## Soil Management Notes
High available water capacity (AWC). Requires low application rate (<8mm/hr) to avoid surface runoff.
