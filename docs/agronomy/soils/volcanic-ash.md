# Volcanic Andosol Soil Dynamics

- **Field Capacity (FC)**: 42.0% volumetric
- **Permanent Wilting Point (PWP)**: 22.0% volumetric
- **Available Water Capacity (AWC)**: 20.0% volumetric
- **Infiltration Rate**: 15.0 mm/hr

## Soil Management Notes
Exceptional porosity and phosphate retention. High organic carbon content buffers moisture depletion.
