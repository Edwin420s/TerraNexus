# Sandy Loam Soil Water Dynamics

- **Field Capacity (FC)**: 22.0% volumetric
- **Permanent Wilting Point (PWP)**: 10.0% volumetric
- **Available Water Capacity (AWC)**: 12.0% volumetric
- **Infiltration Rate**: 25.0 mm/hr

## Soil Management Notes
Rapid infiltration rate (25mm/hr). Demands frequent, shorter drip pulse irrigation cycles.
