# Bell Pepper (Capsicum annuum) Agronomic & Irrigation Profile

- **FAO-56 Crop Coefficients**: Initial Kc = 0.4, Mid-Season Kc = 1.05, Late Season Kc = 0.85
- **Management Allowed Depletion (MAD)**: 0.35
- **Effective Root Zone Depth**: 0.5 - 0.8m
- **Preferred Soil Texture**: Warm fertile loam
- **Target Soil Matric Potential**: -25 to -35 kPa

## Agronomic Guidance
Moisture stress triggers flower drop and sunscald. Uniform drip irrigation ensures thick pericarp wall development.
