# French Beans (Phaseolus vulgaris) Agronomic & Irrigation Profile

- **FAO-56 Crop Coefficients**: Initial Kc = 0.35, Mid-Season Kc = 1.05, Late Season Kc = 0.9
- **Management Allowed Depletion (MAD)**: 0.45
- **Effective Root Zone Depth**: 0.4 - 0.6m
- **Preferred Soil Texture**: Friable loam
- **Target Soil Matric Potential**: -25 to -40 kPa

## Agronomic Guidance
Critical moisture stage at flowering and pod elongation. Inadequate water results in curved, unmarketable pods.
