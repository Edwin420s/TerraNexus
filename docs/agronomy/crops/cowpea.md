# Cowpea (Vigna unguiculata) Agronomic & Irrigation Profile

- **FAO-56 Crop Coefficients**: Initial Kc = 0.35, Mid-Season Kc = 1.0, Late Season Kc = 0.55
- **Management Allowed Depletion (MAD)**: 0.6
- **Effective Root Zone Depth**: 0.6 - 1.0m
- **Preferred Soil Texture**: Tolerant of poor sandy soils
- **Target Soil Matric Potential**: -45 to -75 kPa

## Agronomic Guidance
Nitrogen-fixing legume highly resilient to semi-arid climates. Supplemental irrigation optimizes green pod harvest.
