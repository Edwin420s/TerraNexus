# Tea (Camellia sinensis) Agronomic & Irrigation Profile

- **FAO-56 Crop Coefficients**: Initial Kc = 0.95, Mid-Season Kc = 1.1, Late Season Kc = 1.0
- **Management Allowed Depletion (MAD)**: 0.35
- **Effective Root Zone Depth**: 0.9 - 1.5m
- **Preferred Soil Texture**: Acidic permeable loam (pH 4.5-5.5)
- **Target Soil Matric Potential**: -20 to -30 kPa

## Agronomic Guidance
High humidity and continuous shallow soil moisture promote tender flush shoots. Severe water stress halts plucking cycles.
