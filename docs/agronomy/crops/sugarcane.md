# Sugarcane (Saccharum officinarum) Agronomic & Irrigation Profile

- **FAO-56 Crop Coefficients**: Initial Kc = 0.4, Mid-Season Kc = 1.25, Late Season Kc = 0.75
- **Management Allowed Depletion (MAD)**: 0.5
- **Effective Root Zone Depth**: 1.2 - 2.0m
- **Preferred Soil Texture**: Heavy fertile alluvial soils
- **Target Soil Matric Potential**: -35 to -50 kPa

## Agronomic Guidance
High biomass requires substantial water throughout formative and grand growth phases. Drying off before harvest elevates brix sucrose content.
