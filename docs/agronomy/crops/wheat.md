# Bread Wheat (Triticum aestivum) Agronomic & Irrigation Profile

- **FAO-56 Crop Coefficients**: Initial Kc = 0.35, Mid-Season Kc = 1.15, Late Season Kc = 0.4
- **Management Allowed Depletion (MAD)**: 0.5
- **Effective Root Zone Depth**: 0.9 - 1.3m
- **Preferred Soil Texture**: Clay loam
- **Target Soil Matric Potential**: -35 to -60 kPa

## Agronomic Guidance
Critical stages occur at tillering and anthesis. Water stress at flowering reduces grain count per spikelet.
