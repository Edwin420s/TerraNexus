# Upland Rice (Oryza sativa) Agronomic & Irrigation Profile

- **FAO-56 Crop Coefficients**: Initial Kc = 0.9, Mid-Season Kc = 1.25, Late Season Kc = 0.9
- **Management Allowed Depletion (MAD)**: 0.25
- **Effective Root Zone Depth**: 0.4 - 0.7m
- **Preferred Soil Texture**: Impermeable clay loam
- **Target Soil Matric Potential**: -15 to -25 kPa

## Agronomic Guidance
Extremely sensitive to water deficit at panicle initiation and flowering. Panicle blasting severity increases with moisture stress.
