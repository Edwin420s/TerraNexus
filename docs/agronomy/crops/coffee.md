# Coffee (Coffea arabica) Agronomic & Irrigation Profile

- **FAO-56 Crop Coefficients**: Initial Kc = 0.9, Mid-Season Kc = 1.05, Late Season Kc = 0.95
- **Management Allowed Depletion (MAD)**: 0.4
- **Effective Root Zone Depth**: 1.2 - 1.8m
- **Preferred Soil Texture**: Deep volcanic soils
- **Target Soil Matric Potential**: -40 to -60 kPa

## Agronomic Guidance
Water deficit initiates floral bud dormancy release. Adequate moisture post-flowering ensures uniform cherry expansion.
