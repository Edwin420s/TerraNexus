# Citrus Orange (Citrus sinensis) Agronomic & Irrigation Profile

- **FAO-56 Crop Coefficients**: Initial Kc = 0.65, Mid-Season Kc = 0.7, Late Season Kc = 0.65
- **Management Allowed Depletion (MAD)**: 0.35
- **Effective Root Zone Depth**: 0.8 - 1.4m
- **Preferred Soil Texture**: Deep well-drained loam (pH 6.0-7.0)
- **Target Soil Matric Potential**: -30 to -45 kPa

## Agronomic Guidance
Water deficit during fruit sizing leads to fruit drop and small unmarketable fruit diameters.
