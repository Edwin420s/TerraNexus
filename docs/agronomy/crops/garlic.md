# Garlic (Allium sativum) Agronomic & Irrigation Profile

- **FAO-56 Crop Coefficients**: Initial Kc = 0.5, Mid-Season Kc = 1.0, Late Season Kc = 0.7
- **Management Allowed Depletion (MAD)**: 0.35
- **Effective Root Zone Depth**: 0.3 - 0.4m
- **Preferred Soil Texture**: Well-drained humus-rich loam
- **Target Soil Matric Potential**: -25 to -35 kPa

## Agronomic Guidance
Consistent soil moisture during clove differentiation prevents single-clove round formation.
