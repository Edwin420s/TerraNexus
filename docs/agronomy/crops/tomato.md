# Tomato (Solanum lycopersicum) Agronomic & Irrigation Profile

- **FAO-56 Crop Coefficients**: Initial Kc = 0.45, Mid-Season Kc = 1.15, Late Season Kc = 0.7
- **Management Allowed Depletion (MAD)**: 0.4
- **Effective Root Zone Depth**: 0.6 - 1.0m
- **Preferred Soil Texture**: Well-drained loam
- **Target Soil Matric Potential**: -25 to -35 kPa

## Agronomic Guidance
Blossom-end rot is induced by irregular water supply impeding calcium uptake during early fruit development.
