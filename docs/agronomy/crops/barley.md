# Malting Barley (Hordeum vulgare) Agronomic & Irrigation Profile

- **FAO-56 Crop Coefficients**: Initial Kc = 0.35, Mid-Season Kc = 1.1, Late Season Kc = 0.35
- **Management Allowed Depletion (MAD)**: 0.55
- **Effective Root Zone Depth**: 0.8 - 1.2m
- **Preferred Soil Texture**: Well-drained loam
- **Target Soil Matric Potential**: -40 to -65 kPa

## Agronomic Guidance
Moderate water stress at grain ripening improves grain protein suitability for malting specifications.
