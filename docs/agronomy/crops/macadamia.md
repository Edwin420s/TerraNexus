# Macadamia Nut (Macadamia integrifolia) Agronomic & Irrigation Profile

- **FAO-56 Crop Coefficients**: Initial Kc = 0.5, Mid-Season Kc = 0.8, Late Season Kc = 0.7
- **Management Allowed Depletion (MAD)**: 0.4
- **Effective Root Zone Depth**: 1.0 - 1.8m
- **Preferred Soil Texture**: Deep well-drained volcanic
- **Target Soil Matric Potential**: -30 to -50 kPa

## Agronomic Guidance
Moisture stress during premature nut drop period causes significant yield loss. Drip fertigation enhances kernel oil content.
