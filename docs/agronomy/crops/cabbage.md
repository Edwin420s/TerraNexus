# Cabbage (Brassica oleracea) Agronomic & Irrigation Profile

- **FAO-56 Crop Coefficients**: Initial Kc = 0.45, Mid-Season Kc = 1.05, Late Season Kc = 0.95
- **Management Allowed Depletion (MAD)**: 0.45
- **Effective Root Zone Depth**: 0.4 - 0.6m
- **Preferred Soil Texture**: Heavy fertile loam
- **Target Soil Matric Potential**: -20 to -35 kPa

## Agronomic Guidance
Head splitting occurs when heavy irrigation follows prolonged dry period during head formation.
