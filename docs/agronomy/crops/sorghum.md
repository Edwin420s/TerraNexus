# Grain Sorghum (Sorghum bicolor) Agronomic & Irrigation Profile

- **FAO-56 Crop Coefficients**: Initial Kc = 0.35, Mid-Season Kc = 1.0, Late Season Kc = 0.55
- **Management Allowed Depletion (MAD)**: 0.55
- **Effective Root Zone Depth**: 1.0 - 1.5m
- **Preferred Soil Texture**: Adaptable clay or sand
- **Target Soil Matric Potential**: -40 to -70 kPa

## Agronomic Guidance
C4 photosynthetic efficiency provides high drought resilience. Watering during boot to grain fill ensures panicle exertion.
