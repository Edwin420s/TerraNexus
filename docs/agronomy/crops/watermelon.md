# Watermelon (Citrullus lanatus) Agronomic & Irrigation Profile

- **FAO-56 Crop Coefficients**: Initial Kc = 0.4, Mid-Season Kc = 1.0, Late Season Kc = 0.75
- **Management Allowed Depletion (MAD)**: 0.4
- **Effective Root Zone Depth**: 0.8 - 1.2m
- **Preferred Soil Texture**: Warm sandy loam
- **Target Soil Matric Potential**: -30 to -45 kPa

## Agronomic Guidance
Critical watering phase occurs between flowering and fruit enlargement. Reduce water 10 days before harvest to concentrate sugar.
