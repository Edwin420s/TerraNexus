# Mango (Mangifera indica) Agronomic & Irrigation Profile

- **FAO-56 Crop Coefficients**: Initial Kc = 0.5, Mid-Season Kc = 0.8, Late Season Kc = 0.7
- **Management Allowed Depletion (MAD)**: 0.45
- **Effective Root Zone Depth**: 1.5 - 2.5m
- **Preferred Soil Texture**: Deep well-aerated loam
- **Target Soil Matric Potential**: -40 to -70 kPa

## Agronomic Guidance
Requires 2-3 months dry stress prior to flowering to promote vegetative dormancy and uniform panicle initiation.
