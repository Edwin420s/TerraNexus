# Maize (Zea mays) Agronomic & Irrigation Profile

- **FAO-56 Crop Coefficients**: Initial Kc = 0.4, Mid-Season Kc = 1.2, Late Season Kc = 0.6
- **Management Allowed Depletion (MAD)**: 0.5
- **Effective Root Zone Depth**: 0.8 - 1.2m
- **Preferred Soil Texture**: Clay loam / Sandy loam
- **Target Soil Matric Potential**: -30 to -50 kPa

## Agronomic Guidance
Tasseling and silking are critical water stress stages. Moisture deficit during pollination induces kernel abortion.
