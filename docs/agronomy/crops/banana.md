# Banana (Musa acuminata) Agronomic & Irrigation Profile

- **FAO-56 Crop Coefficients**: Initial Kc = 1.0, Mid-Season Kc = 1.2, Late Season Kc = 1.1
- **Management Allowed Depletion (MAD)**: 0.35
- **Effective Root Zone Depth**: 0.5 - 0.9m
- **Preferred Soil Texture**: Rich organic alluvial loam
- **Target Soil Matric Potential**: -20 to -30 kPa

## Agronomic Guidance
High water consumer. Water deficit slows leaf emission, delays bunch emergence, and reduces bunch finger count.
