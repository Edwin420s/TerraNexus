# Irish Potato (Solanum tuberosum) Agronomic & Irrigation Profile

- **FAO-56 Crop Coefficients**: Initial Kc = 0.5, Mid-Season Kc = 1.15, Late Season Kc = 0.75
- **Management Allowed Depletion (MAD)**: 0.35
- **Effective Root Zone Depth**: 0.4 - 0.6m
- **Preferred Soil Texture**: Loose sandy loam
- **Target Soil Matric Potential**: -25 to -35 kPa

## Agronomic Guidance
Moisture fluctuations cause tuber cracking and secondary growth. Uniform moisture is mandatory during tuber initiation.
