# Sunflower (Helianthus annuus) Agronomic & Irrigation Profile

- **FAO-56 Crop Coefficients**: Initial Kc = 0.35, Mid-Season Kc = 1.1, Late Season Kc = 0.45
- **Management Allowed Depletion (MAD)**: 0.45
- **Effective Root Zone Depth**: 1.0 - 1.8m
- **Preferred Soil Texture**: Deep sandy clay loam
- **Target Soil Matric Potential**: -40 to -60 kPa

## Agronomic Guidance
Deep taproot extracts subsoil reserves. Flowering to achene filling requires reliable moisture to optimize seed oil percentage.
